4At a Glance

Summary

IT portfolio pilot is designed to establish a common, scalable approach to portfolio management, creating consistent governance, data, delivery practices, and reporting across IT teams. The following outlines the target vision and the implementation flow used to move toward it.

Visions

  • Unified Portfolio Structure: Established a consistent portfolio structure across IT teams, enabling information to be captured and managed in a common format.
  • Improved Data Quality: Cleaned, consolidated, and standardized portfolio data, removing deprecated and duplicate information.
  • Consistent Portfolio Governance: Established common standards, terminology, and guidelines to support consistent portfolio management practices.
  • Aligned Delivery Model: Created a unified lifecycle model that enables consistent tracking and comparison of different types of delivery work.
  • Improved Visibility & Reporting: Established the foundation for consistent portfolio reporting and dashboards across strategic, customer, program, product, and delivery perspectives.
  • Improved Customer & Stakeholder Communication: Introduced standardized charter templates to create clearer and more consistent communication with customers and stakeholders across projects, products, and programs.

Implementation Flow

The pilot follows a progressive approach, moving from portfolio definition to operationalization, insight generation, and continuous improvement.

Definition

Identify current gaps and define the portfolio information needed to support management and delivery.

Foundation

Establish the common lifecycle, attributes, terminology, and information structure required for portfolio management.

Operationalization

Implement the portfolio structure in Jira through standardized workflows, fields, and views, then analyze the captured data through management and delivery reports to generate insights and support informed decision-making.

Value Cycle

Continuously capture, review, learn, and improve portfolio insights.
