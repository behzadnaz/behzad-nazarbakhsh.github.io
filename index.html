06 / ABOUT

Connecting strategy, product, engineering, and organizational change.

I help software organizations turn strategic goals into products, delivery systems, and organizational capabilities that create measurable business value.

I work at the intersection of software engineering, product management, Agile ways of working, IT portfolio management, and organizational change. I help leaders and teams clarify priorities, improve how work flows from strategy to delivery, and build systems that support sustainable performance.

My approach combines software craftsmanship and product thinking with practical management and organizational practices. The goal is not simply to deliver software faster, but to create clearer decisions, stronger execution, and better outcomes for the organization.
