HOW I WORK

The lens I bring to every challenge.

  • See the whole systemLocal fixes rarely improve a system. I look at how people, products, technology, quality and structure interact, so improvement targets the real constraint rather than a single team or process.
  • Follow the work end to endI help teams and organizations trace the path from customer need through product, engineering and quality to feedback, removing waste and delay so every output serves an outcome that creates business value.
  • Bring AI into the pictureAI speeds up coding, but speed alone is not value. I help teams rethink engineering with clear problem framing, guardrails and human judgment, keeping quality built in and collaboration strong.
  • Work where there's no simple answerComplex problems call for short feedback loops, not big plans. I bring business, product, engineering, quality and leadership perspectives together, run small experiments, and turn what we learn into agreed actions.

What I do

I’m a product coach and Agile expert. I help IT organizations, in B2B, B2C and beyond, create the conditions where people and teams can deliver better software and lasting value. My work spans organizational design, software delivery, product development, quality, engineering, and leadership.

Where I’ve done it

I’ve worked alongside senior managers, leadership teams, and delivery teams, in organizations at every stage of growth, from setting up effective ways of working to untangling complex delivery challenges. Most recently, I piloted IT portfolio management and organizational change management in organizations that had not practiced them before.

Where I start

I focus on how organizations structure work, make decisions, collaborate, manage quality, and keep improving. My approach: start with the context, understand the system, and change it together with the people in it, so the change lasts.
