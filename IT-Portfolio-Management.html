SELECTED WORK · IT PORTFOLIO MANAGEMENT

IT Portfolio Management

Turning IT strategy into transparent, prioritized, and value-driven execution across IT and the business.

20 FEBRUARY 2026 — 19 AUGUST 2026

Report to the CIO

This report documents the establishment and operationalization of IT Portfolio Management as a new capability, including portfolio governance, shared terminology, standardized Program/Product/Project Charters, aligned delivery lifecycle practices, Jira-based portfolio data, reporting, and stakeholder visibility.

The work covered ten portfolios and included 47 working sessions with nine delivery teams. The portfolio environment was established as a common structure for capturing, comparing, and managing delivery information.

Key focus areas include unified portfolio structure, improved data quality, consistent governance, aligned delivery models, improved visibility and reporting, and clearer customer and stakeholder communication.
