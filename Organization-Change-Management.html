Organization Change Management

Turning organizational change into successful adoption, sustainable outcomes, and measurable business value across IT and the business.

1 APRIL 2024 — 1 MARCH 2025

Report to the CIO

This report documents Organization Change Management work focused on connecting organizational change with adoption, stakeholder engagement, business value, and sustainable outcomes.

The work includes stakeholder recognition, business value and benefits realization, communication strategy and execution, key- and end-user profile management, and project business objectives clarification.

The report demonstrates how change management activities were connected to broader IT and business objectives rather than treated as a separate communication activity.
